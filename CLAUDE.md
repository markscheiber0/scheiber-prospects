## Email drafting rules

When drafting any outreach or email on Mark's behalf:

- **Never use em dashes (`—`) or en dashes (`–`).** They are a tell that an email was AI-written. Use periods, commas, or restructure the sentence instead.
- Sign off with the contact email **mark.scheiber@scheiberintelligence.com**.
- Keep cold emails short, specific to the prospect's signal, and end with a low-friction "just reply" call to action.
- Include a soft opt-out line so outreach stays CAN-SPAM friendly.
